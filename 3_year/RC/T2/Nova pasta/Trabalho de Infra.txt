SALA 1:
No switch:
	(Criar a VLAN 10)
	
	Switch>enable 
	Switch#configure terminal
	Enter configuration commands, one per line.  End with CNTL/Z.
	Switch(config)#vlan 10
	Switch(config-vlan)#name SALA1
	Switch(config-vlan)#exit
	Switch(config)#do wr
	Building configuration...
	[OK]
	
	(Atribuir as portas que conectarão os dispositivos com fio à VALN 10, fiz uso das FastEthernet 0/1 a 0/4)
	
	Switch(config)#interface range FastEthernet 0/1 - 4
	Switch(config-if-range)#switchport mode access
	Switch(config-if-range)#switchport access vlan 10
	Switch(config-if-range)#exit
	Switch(config)#do wr
	
	(Configurar a vlan do Telefone IP, coloquei meu telefone na FastEthernet 0/2)
	
	Switch(config)#interface FastEthernet 0/2
	Switch(config-if)#switchport voice vlan 10
	Switch(config-if)#exit
	Switch(config)#do wr
	Building configuration...
	[OK]
	
	(Nota: as portas que se conectarão aos switches multilayer futuramente deverão se configuradas como trunk)
