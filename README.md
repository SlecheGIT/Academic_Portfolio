# 🎓 Academic Repository
This repository contains the projects, exercises, and lecture notes developed during my Computer Science degree at UEMS. The goal is to document my progress in programming logic, data structures, and especially my interest in low-level systems.

---

## 📂 Repository Structure

The Repository is organized by academic years:

### 👨‍🏫 Teaching & Community Leadership
* **Python & Automation Instructor:** Currently serving as a teacher in a university project, instructing students on Python logic and automation scripts.
* **Web Development Instructor:** Previously led courses on **JavaScript, HTML, and CSS**, helping students build their first web-based applications.

  
### 📅 Year 1: Foundations
* **Algorithms and Data Structures I:** Introduction to computational thinking, variables, and fundamental control structures.
* **Python & Java:** Intensive exercises and mini-courses focused on mastering syntax and logic in **Python** and **Java**.
* **Calculus I and Calculus II**

  
### 📅 Year 2: Systems & Advanced Algorithms
* **Algorithms and Data Structures II:** Advanced study and implementation of **data compression (Huffman coding)**, complex **sorting algorithms**, **balanced trees** (AVL, B), and fundamental structures like **Linked Lists, Stacks, and Queues**.
* **Programming Paradigms:** Comparative study of programming styles, with a heavy focus on **Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) in C++**.
* **Computer Architecture:** Advanced analysis of CPU design and the **Instruction Set Architecture (ISA)**. Focused on performance optimization through **instruction pipelining**, **register** allocation, and **memory hierarchy** management (Cache, RAM). Includes studies on **processor speed**, **chip efficiency**, **latency**, and **throughput**.
* **Numerical Methods:** Computational mathematics focusing on algorithms like **Gauss Elimination** and the **Newton-Raphson Method**.
* **Formal Languages & Automata Theory:** Mathematical foundations of computation, covering **Deterministic Finite Automata (DFA)**, Nondeterministic Finite Automata (NFA), and **Turing Machines**.


### 📅 Year 3: Infrastructure & Theory
* **Analysis of Algorithms:** Advanced study of design using **Divide & Conquer, Dynamic Programming, Greedy Algorithms, Induction**, **Big O notation** and **NP-Completeness**.
* **Operating Systems (OS):** Study of kernels and resource management, including **semaphores, processes, multi-threading, and CPU schedulers**.
* **Formal Languages & Automata Theory:** Mathematical foundations of computation, covering **Deterministic Finite Automata (DFA)**, Nondeterministic Finite Automata (NFA), and **Turing Machines**.
* **Database Systems:** Relational modeling, SQL optimization, and data persistence.
* **Computer Networks:** Study of communication protocols and network architecture. 
    * **OSI Model:** Full analysis of the **7 Layers** (Physical to Application).
    * **Transport Protocols:** Implementation and comparison of **TCP** and **UDP**
* **Assembly Language (MIPS):** Low-level programming using the **MIPS architecture** and simulators like **MARS**:
    * Implementation of **arithmetic and logic operations** directly on registers.
    * Study of **Instruction Set Architecture (ISA)** and R, I, and J-type instruction formats.
    * Memory management using **Load/Store** instructions and stack pointer manipulation.
    * Development of procedures, leaf functions, and recursive calls in assembly.
